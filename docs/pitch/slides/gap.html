Maps answer how fast. At night she also needs to know what’s along the way.

Is it lit?

Streetlights, lamps, the glow of the area from space

Are people around?

Shops, cafés, pharmacies still open at this hour; transit nearby

Where’s help?

How far to a police station, hospital or open place if she needs one

Today women answer these from memory, rumour, or by calling someone and walking fast. The data to answer them exists; nobody has put it together for this question.
