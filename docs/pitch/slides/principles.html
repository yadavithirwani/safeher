Responsible by design

Honest about what data can and can’t tell her

Never promises safety

“More support”, “better lit”, “weak stretch”. Never “safe” or “dangerous”, enforced in code.

Doesn’t stigmatise areas

City-wide crime figures are context only. They never colour a street they can’t be tied to.

Says when it’s guessing

Estimated lighting and assumed opening hours are labelled. Low-confidence data says so.

Her location stays hers

Sharing is opt-in, keeps no history and ends when she says. Route explanations never see her live GPS.

No silent fallbacks: if a key or model fails, the app shows the error rather than quietly giving a worse answer.
